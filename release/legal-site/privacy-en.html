Data stored on your device

The app stores your selected learning day, progress for each of the seven days, conversation answers, feedback, known words and completion times locally. Each day retains at most 100 conversation messages. It also stores your app language, voice choice, speaking pace, daily goal, onboarding completion, last learning-moment date and optional reminder settings. Your device language is used for the initial language choice. Do not enter confidential information or anyone else’s personal data in exercises.

Purposes and legal bases

Local processing enables your chosen lessons, relevant feedback and resuming later. Where personal data is involved, core functions rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in providing the local learning app you choose to use. There is no advertising, profiling or automatic transmission of your answers, and you can delete learning data at any time. Storage access strictly necessary for the expressly requested function is based on section 25(2)(2) of Germany’s TDDDG. You activate optional reminders yourself; where consent is required, it is based on Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and can be withdrawn at any time by switching reminders off. For users in the United Kingdom, the corresponding UK GDPR bases apply where applicable.

No accounts, advertising or external AI

PhraseUNO has no user account, operator backend, cloud synchronisation, advertising, analytics or tracking services. Learning and conversation checks run locally using fixed rules. Lucía is simulated; your answers are not sent to generative AI. We do not sell or rent your learning data or use it for targeted advertising. There are no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Listening, microphone and on-device speech recognition

Lucía reads fixed Spanish lesson texts using a device voice. When you tap Repeat aloud or Speak your answer, the app asks for microphone access. During the visible recording, on-device speech recognition converts your voice to text; no audio file is saved. The app requires on-device recognition and does not automatically switch to an online service. If Spanish offline recognition is unavailable, you can listen again, practise aloud yourself or optionally write. Recognised conversation answers are saved in local progress like typed answers; repetition transcripts appear only temporarily on screen. Word checking is not pronunciation assessment. You can stop recording and revoke microphone access in system settings; leaving the exercise or moving the app to the background cancels recording. This optional microphone processing is based on your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. System-provider information also applies to voice/language model downloads and optional keyboard dictation. Two fixed recording voices are not currently included.

Optional learning reminders

Reminders are off initially. Notification permission is requested only when you activate them. At most one general reminder is scheduled daily at your chosen device time, without sound or an app badge. It contains no conversation answers or progress. The app uses neither push tokens nor a notification server for this. Switch reminders off in app settings or revoke permission in your operating system.

Retention and deletion

Learning data stays on your device until you use Legal & your data → Delete learning data or clear app data through the operating system. In-app deletion removes progress and conversations for all learning days; personal settings are preserved. You can change settings; all local app data can be removed through the operating system. App storage has no additional app-specific encryption. Depending on your operating system settings, device or cloud backups may contain copies. In-app deletion does not remove those externally managed copies.

Sharing and operating system backups

Share learning data passes your full local learning record to the share menu. Data is shared only when you choose a destination and complete the action. Check the content and recipient first. The app does not automatically send anything to the operator. Your chosen recipients, device backups and their storage locations are additionally subject to the relevant services’ terms.

When you contact us

If you email us, we process your sender address, message and information you choose to provide to answer your enquiry. This is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in handling your request. Communication uses the relevant email services; please avoid sending learning records or confidential information. After resolution we normally delete the enquiry within six months, unless legal retention duties or specific legal claims require otherwise.

Your privacy rights

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, you have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability subject to the relevant conditions. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests for reasons relating to your particular situation. Consent may be withdrawn for the future. Contact phrase_uno@gmx.de. We cannot access local storage unless you choose to send it to us. You may complain to a competent authority, such as LDI NRW (https://www.ldi.nrw.de/) or the UK ICO (https://ico.org.uk/). Other statutory rights remain unaffected.

Teenagers and international use

The app is aimed at adults and older teenagers aged 16 and over, and is not directed at children under 13. There are no social profiles, location requests, advertising or behavioural profiles. No age or identity documents are requested. The service is operated from Germany. The operator does not transmit local learning records to recipients outside your device; separately consider your chosen system services, email services, sharing and backups.
