Usage information

Updated: 9 November 2026

Scope and audience

PhraseUNO contains seven Spanish learning days for everyday life and travel. Each day combines phrase building, a simulated conversation and three vocabulary cards. The app is aimed at adults and older teenagers aged 16 and over. The current version has no purchases, subscriptions or accounts.

Simulation and learning outcomes

Lucía responds using fixed conversation rules. She is not connected to generative AI. The app checks typed answers, not pronunciation. Audio currently uses operating system voices. Exercises are learning aids, not an accredited qualification. No particular learning outcome or speed is promised.

Practise safely

Use only the supplied practice information. Ana in the hotel conversation is a fictional practice name. Do not enter personal or confidential information. Use the app only when safe, and not while driving. Progress is stored locally and does not automatically transfer to a new device.
