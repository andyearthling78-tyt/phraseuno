Your first Spanish phrases for everyday situations.

PhraseUNO guides you through seven short learning days: at the café, bakery, restaurant, finding your way, market, hotel and asking for help.

Build a phrase step by step, practise four answers with simulated teacher Lucía and reinforce three words with flip cards. Each day is designed for around ten minutes: 80% phrases and conversation, 20% word practice. Learn at your own pace.

Included:
• Seven learning days with separate progress
• German or English explanations
• Spanish audio examples using available device voices
• A daily goal and optional silent learning reminder
• Gentle, encouraging learning moments
• Local progress storage without an account
• No advertising, purchases or subscriptions

Conversations are local simulations with fixed responses and hints. There is no open-ended AI conversation or pronunciation assessment. Listen to Lucía, repeat aloud and answer using the microphone. Spanish on-device recognition requires a compatible offline language model and microphone permission. Writing is optional. Each learning day opens optional vocabulary practice that can be deferred. The weekly collection brings all learned words together; the weekly review requires practising them all.

Lesson content is stored on your device. Depending on your operating system, speech voices may require downloads or a connection. Progress does not automatically transfer between devices.

PhraseUNO is a one-week introduction, not a complete language course. For adults and older teenagers aged 16 and over.
